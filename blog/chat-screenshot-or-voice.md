# Chat, screenshot, or voice

Wiggle gives you three choices each time it opens: Chat, Screenshot, or Voice. You pick one. A screenshot is not taken in the background, and voice is not an always-on microphone.

By Vicki, Noaman & Najia · October 25, 2026
## Key points

- Three choices, every time Wiggle opens.
- Chat is a written question.
- Screenshot is a picture of the screen you chose to take.
- Voice is a spoken question.
- None of the three runs until you pick it.

## Picking one
The three buttons are the whole decision. You do not set a background mode and leave it. The next time you shake, you pick again.

Use Chat when the words are enough. Use Screenshot when the answer depends on what is drawn on screen. Use Voice when you would rather say it. The privacy guides cover screenshots (/blog/screenshots/) and voice (/blog/voice/) on their own.

## Chat
Chat is for asking in writing about whatever is on screen, without leaving the spot where you got stuck.

## Screenshot
Screenshot is for the question that needs the screen. You choose it. It is not taken for you while you work. There is more in Does a Wiggle screenshot leave your Mac? (/blog/screenshots/)

## Voice
Voice is a mode you pick, the same way you pick Chat or Screenshot. It is how you ask out loud. It is not a microphone that stays on. See Does Wiggle listen all the time? (/blog/voice/)

## Questions
Do I have to use all three? No. Each time Wiggle opens, you pick the one that fits the question.

Does the website preview capture my screen? No. The preview on this site says so in the dialog, and it does not listen either.
