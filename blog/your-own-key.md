# What happens when you use your own Claude or OpenAI key

If you choose your own Claude or OpenAI key, that question goes to the provider you picked. Apple Intelligence and a local Ollama model run on your Mac and do not take that path.

By Noaman · October 23, 2026
## Key points

- The key is one you already have for Claude or OpenAI.
- Wiggle uses it to send the question you asked.
- That request goes to the provider, because that is their API.
- This website never asks you to paste a key.

## What we do not do with it
A key you use stays with you. Wiggle does not ask this website for a key, and it does not keep a second copy.

If you never choose that path, the question stays with Apple Intelligence or with Ollama. Those do not use your Claude or OpenAI key.

## What “your own key” means
Wiggle can call Claude or OpenAI with a key you already have. Wiggle is not selling you a seat on those services. The request is yours.

## What leaves the Mac
The question you send with that key leaves the Mac, because that is how those APIs work. Apple Intelligence and Ollama do not use that path.

This site does not ask for your API key.

## What we list
Jabble’s promise for Wiggle is that anything the app sends is listed for you to see. A key you never hand over is not sent.

## Questions
Can I use Wiggle without a key? Yes. Apple Intelligence, on a Mac that has it, or a local model through Ollama, does not need your Claude or OpenAI key.
