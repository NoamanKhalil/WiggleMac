# Record a workflow and play it back

Choose Workflow, press Record, and Wiggle saves the clicks, typing, and shortcuts you make. Play that workflow back 1 to 99 times. The file stays on your Mac.

By Noaman · November 2, 2026
## Key points

- Workflow is the fourth choice. The key is W.
- It records clicks, double-clicks, right-clicks, drags, scrolling, typing, and shortcuts.
- Play a saved workflow 1 to 99 times, at the speed you recorded.
- The file stays on your Mac and is never sent anywhere.
- It replays fixed spots. It does not use AI, and it does not adapt.

## Picking Workflow
Shake the pointer to open Wiggle, then choose Workflow, or press W. Chat, Screenshot, and Voice are still there. Workflow is the one that records what you do and plays it back. The other three are in [Chat, screenshot, or voice](/blog/chat-screenshot-or-voice/).

## Record it once
Press Record and the ring around the pointer turns red. Wiggle records clicks, double-clicks, right-clicks, drags, scrolling, typing, and keyboard shortcuts in any app. A small bar shows how many steps are recorded.

Esc pauses and resumes. Nothing you do while paused is recorded, and that Esc is not recorded or passed on to the app. Wiggle again, press stop on the bar, or use the menu bar to stop. Then name the workflow, remove any stray steps, and save. Typed text shows as one step, such as Type “hello”.

macOS withholds keystrokes typed into password fields, so passwords are never recorded. Esc cannot be recorded as a step.

## Play it back
Press play next to a saved workflow and run it 1 to 99 times in a row. It replays the same spots, at the speed you recorded. A small bar shows progress, such as “Step 3 of 14 · run 2 of 5”, and places itself away from the spots the workflow clicks.

Esc pauses between steps, and Esc again resumes. A wiggle, or the stop button, stops the run. A run also stops if a display is connected or disconnected, or the Mac goes to sleep. Rename a workflow with the pencil button or by double-clicking the name, or delete it.

## Where it is stored
A workflow stays on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Wiggle/workflows.json, and is never sent anywhere. Workflow needs Accessibility. macOS asks for it the first time you record or run a workflow.

## What it will not do
A workflow replays fixed screen positions, so windows need to be where they were when you recorded. It does not use AI, read the screen, or adapt to changes. It does not record audio.

## Questions
What if a workflow goes wrong? Esc pauses between steps. Esc again resumes. A wiggle, or the stop button, stops the run.

Does this website record my pointer? No. The preview and the films do not record, replay, or move your pointer.
