# The parody pitch is not the product

The page at wigglemac.com/satire.html is a parody pitch. The Mac app is real. The billing, the numbers, and the ethics on that page are not.

By Vicki, Noaman & Najia · October 18, 2026
## Key points

- The Mac app is real. The pitch at /satire.html is a parody.
- The shake, the rainbow cursor, screenshots, and voice are real.
- The prices, the hostage cursor, the resale of screenshots, the always-on mic, and the manager scoreboard are not.

## How to tell which page you are on
The product pages are this one, the home page, and the short legal pages. They do not ask you to pay. The parody page says “parody pitch” in a banner at the top and links back to the product.

If a sentence names a price, a surge, a daily total, or a note to your manager, it is from the parody. It is not a feature list.

## What is real
The shake, the rainbow cursor, screenshots, and voice work in the Mac app. So do Apple Intelligence, Ollama, and your own Claude or OpenAI key.

## What is the joke

- A price that rises with how fast you shook.
- A cursor you pay to get back.
- Screenshots kept forever and licensed to advertisers.
- A microphone that listens to everything.
- A score sent to your manager.
- The daily totals and the enterprise pilots.

None of those are offers. Terms (/terms.html) says the same thing in shorter form.

## Where to read it
The parody stays up on purpose, at satire.html (/satire.html), with a banner that points back here. If you only wanted the joke, that is the page. If you wanted the app, you are already in the right place.

## Questions
Why keep the parody online? It was the first page, and people still have the link. It stays labeled as a parody so it cannot be mistaken for a price list.
