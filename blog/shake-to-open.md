# How to open Wiggle by shaking the mouse

Shake the pointer back and forth five times, quickly, in under a second. Slow moves and drags are ignored. Wiggle then appears at the cursor.

By Noaman · October 26, 2026
## Key points

- Five back-and-forth strokes.
- All five inside one second.
- Each stroke has to be a real move, not a twitch.
- A slow drag is ignored.
- After it opens, there is a short pause before the next shake counts.

## Why it ignores ordinary movement
A gesture that fired on every flick would open Wiggle while you were just working. The window is short on purpose, and a stroke has to travel far enough to count. That is why a careful move across a window does nothing, and a quick shake does.

The rainbow arrow is the sign that the gesture landed. Wiggle is then at that spot. You have not been taken to another screen.

## The gesture
Wiggle is looking for a shake, not for ordinary mouse movement. The rule is the same one the app uses: five reversals, each a real stroke, inside a short window.

- Move the pointer back and forth five times.
- Do it in under a second.
- A slow drag, or a careful move across a window, does not count.

## What you see
Your pointer turns into a rainbow arrow, and Wiggle opens at that spot. You are still looking at the same place on screen. Pick Chat, Screenshot, or Voice.

On this website, the same gesture opens a preview. That preview does not send anything. The Mac app is the one that answers.

## If it does not open
Shake faster, not wider. A long slow sweep is still one move. If you are on a trackpad, a few short flicks work better than one big swipe.
