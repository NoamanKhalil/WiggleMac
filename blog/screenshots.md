# Does a Wiggle screenshot leave your Mac?

Wiggle takes a screenshot when you choose Screenshot, not in the background. On-device models keep that question on the Mac. Your own Claude or OpenAI key sends that question to the provider you chose. Screenshots are not sold.

By Noaman · October 22, 2026
## Key points

- A screenshot is a button you press for one question.
- It is not taken while you work.
- On-device models keep that question on the Mac.
- Your own key sends the question you chose to that provider.
- Screenshots are not a product we license.

## How to tell
If you did not choose Screenshot, there is no screenshot. The preview on wigglemac.com is even narrower: it does not capture the screen at all, and the dialog says so.


## You choose it
Screenshot is one of the three buttons, next to Chat and Voice. If you do not pick it, Wiggle does not grab the screen for you.

The preview on this website does not capture your screen at all.

## Where it goes
With Apple Intelligence or Ollama, the model is on your Mac. With your own Claude or OpenAI key, the question you chose to send goes to that provider.

A screenshot is for the question you chose. Wiggle does not keep every screenshot or license it to advertisers.

## Questions
Does Wiggle watch the screen all day? No. A screenshot is a choice you make for one question.
