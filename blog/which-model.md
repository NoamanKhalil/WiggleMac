# Apple Intelligence, Ollama, or your own key

Wiggle works with Apple Intelligence, a local model through Ollama, or your own Claude or OpenAI key. The first two run on your Mac. A question you send with your own key goes to that provider.

By Vicki, Noaman & Najia · October 24, 2026
## Key points

- Apple Intelligence runs on the Mac, on a Mac that has it.
- Ollama runs a local model you already have.
- Your own Claude or OpenAI key sends that question to the provider you chose.
- There is no Wiggle account in front of any of the three.

## How to choose
Stay on the Mac when the question can be answered there. Apple Intelligence and Ollama are that path. Use your own key when you want Claude or OpenAI specifically, and accept that those companies receive the question you send. The detail is in what your own key sends (/blog/your-own-key/).

Wiggle does not rank the three, and it does not publish a speed or a score for them. The difference that matters here is where the question goes.

## Apple Intelligence
On a Mac that has Apple Intelligence, Wiggle can use Apple’s on-device model. That model runs on the Mac.

## Ollama
If you already run a local model through Ollama, Wiggle can use that. The model stays on the machine.

## Your own key
Bring a Claude or OpenAI key when you want those models. That question goes to the provider you chose, because you asked it to. What your own key sends (/blog/your-own-key/) is the longer version.

Wiggle does not publish a fourth, secret model, and it does not invent a number the way a monitor might. It answers the question you asked.

## Questions
Which Macs have Apple Intelligence? Apple decides that, and it depends on the Mac and the version of macOS. Wiggle does not add a second list.

Do I have to sign in to pick a model? No. There is no Wiggle account. A key, if you use one, is the key you already have for that provider.
