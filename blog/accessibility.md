# Workflow needs Accessibility

Workflow needs Accessibility. macOS asks for it the first time you record or run a workflow. That permission is what lets a workflow click and type for you.

By Noaman · November 16, 2026
## Key points

- Workflow needs Accessibility.
- macOS asks the first time you record or run a workflow.
- That permission is what lets a workflow click, type, and use shortcuts.
- Chat, Screenshot, and Voice are the other choices. The prompt is for Workflow.
- The file still stays on your Mac, and passwords are still withheld.

## When macOS asks
Shake the pointer, choose Workflow, or press W, then press Record. The first time you record, macOS asks for Accessibility. It asks the same way the first time you press play on a saved workflow.

Allow it. Recording and playback can then click, double-click, right-click, drag, scroll, type, and use keyboard shortcuts. If you dismissed the prompt, macOS keeps the switch in System Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Accessibility. Turn Wiggle on, then record or press play again.

## What it is for
A workflow plays clicks and typing back in other apps. macOS calls that Accessibility, and it asks before Wiggle can do it. Chat, Screenshot, and Voice stay the other three choices. Those are a written question, a picture you chose, or a spoken question. How to record is in [Record a workflow and play it back](/blog/workflow/).

## What it does not change
Allowing Accessibility does not make a workflow follow a window that moved. It still aims at the same spots, at the speed you recorded, from 1 to 99 times. It does not use AI, read the screen, or adapt. It does not record audio. That limit is in [A workflow replays the same spots](/blog/same-spots/).

macOS still withholds keystrokes typed into password fields, so passwords are never recorded. The file stays on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Wiggle/workflows.json, and is never sent anywhere.

Esc pauses between steps. Esc again resumes. A wiggle, or the stop button, stops the run.

## Questions
What if I already said no? Turn Wiggle on under Accessibility in System Settings, then record or press play again.

Does this website need that permission? No. The preview and the films do not record, replay, or move your pointer.
