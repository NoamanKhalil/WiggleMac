# What is Wiggle for Mac?

Wiggle is an early-access Mac app. Shake the mouse and it appears at the cursor, so you can chat, take a screenshot, or ask out loud about what is on screen.

By Noaman · October 27, 2026
## Key points

- Wiggle is a Mac app in early access, made by Jabble.
- Shake the pointer and it opens at the cursor.
- You then pick Chat, Screenshot, or Voice.
- The model is Apple Intelligence, Ollama on your Mac, or your own Claude or OpenAI key.
- This website is the explanation. The buttons here are a preview, not the app.

## A normal question
You are in the middle of something. The pointer is already on the thing you do not understand. Shaking opens Wiggle there, instead of sending you to a chat window in another app.

The rest of the guide set is the detail: how the shake works (/blog/shake-to-open/), the three ways to ask (/blog/chat-screenshot-or-voice/), and which model answers (/blog/which-model/).

## What it is
Wiggle is the assistant for the moment you are stuck. You do not go looking for a chat window. You shake the pointer, and Wiggle opens where the pointer already is.

From there you pick one of three ways to ask: Chat, Screenshot, or Voice. The model can be Apple Intelligence, a local model through Ollama, or your own Claude or OpenAI key.
