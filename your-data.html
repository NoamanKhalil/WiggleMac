Your data

This website does not have accounts. Loading a page does not create a Wiggle profile. The only message we receive is an email you send yourself to hello@jabble.si.

On the Mac, Apple Intelligence and Ollama run locally. Your own Claude or OpenAI key sends that question to the provider you picked. The app is built with no analytics.

The parody page’s lines about keeping screenshots forever, listening to everything, and sending a score to your manager are not what the app does.
