How it works

Shake. Pick. Ask.

No shortcut to remember and no window to find. Wiggle shows up where your attention already is, and gets out of the way when you're done.

Step 1

Wiggle your mouse

A few quick back-and-forth strokes, and a spinning rainbow ring appears around your pointer. Small jitters, slow moves and drags are ignored.

Chat Screenshot Voice
Step 2

Pick Chat, Screenshot or Voice

A small menu opens right next to your cursor. Click one, or press C, S or V.

Step 3

Ask, and keep going

The answer streams in next to your cursor. Ask follow-ups in the same thread, then wiggle again to close.

What it can do

Everything you need, at your cursor.

Ask about your screen

Screenshot captures the display under your cursor and attaches it to your question. Wiggle's own windows and your pointer are left out.

Just say it

Voice turns your question into text on your Mac and sends it when you pause. Say “screenshot” first and it captures the screen too.

Chat with follow-ups

Replies stream in as they're written. Follow-up questions continue the same conversation until you close Wiggle.

Your choice of model

Apple Intelligence works out of the box. Pro adds local models through Ollama, and Claude or OpenAI with your own key.

Keep the screenshot

Click Save to keep a capture as a full-resolution PNG in your screenshots folder. Otherwise it's gone when Wiggle closes.

Stays out of the way

Lives in the menu bar, with no Dock icon. Adjust how hard you need to wiggle, turn it off for a while, or launch it at login.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an API key?

No. Wiggle works out of the box with Apple Intelligence on your Mac. You only need a key if you want to use Claude or OpenAI, which are part of Pro.

Does anything leave my Mac?

Not with Apple Intelligence or a local model. If you choose Claude or OpenAI, your question and any screenshot you attach go to that provider, using your key. Wiggle itself has no servers and no analytics.

Will it open by accident?

It takes several fast back-and-forth strokes within a second. Small jitters, slow movements and drags are ignored. You can make it more or less sensitive from the menu bar, or switch it off.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. On macOS 26, Apple Intelligence reads the text in your screenshots; on macOS 27 it sees the whole image. Vision models through Ollama, Claude and OpenAI see the image on either.

What permissions does it ask for?

Screen Recording the first time you take a screenshot, Microphone and Speech Recognition the first time you use Voice, and access to your screenshots folder the first time you save one. Each is asked only when you use that feature.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase for up to 3 of your own Macs, with free updates for all of version 1. You can try it free for 14 days first.
